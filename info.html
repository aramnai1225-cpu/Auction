About Our Platform

Our "Court Seized Asset Auction Platform" is designed to make court-seized asset auctions transparent, fair, and accessible to the public. It is managed under the guidelines of the Government Execution Authority. Users can visit the official website to learn more about the organization.

  • Open to all registered users
